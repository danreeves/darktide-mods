# PingMonitor Changelog

## 26.11.02

Added `Always show during gameplay`, off by default, to keep your ping on screen without opening the tactical overlay. Suggested by mad3mad.
Added `Averaging samples`, 30 by default, to set how many measurements your ping is averaged over.
Added `Widget position` settings to move your ping anywhere on screen. Suggested by lumberfart and feisheng68.
Changed the default position of your ping from the centre of the screen to the top centre. Suggested by feisheng68.
Fixed the ping icon and number having no colour when jitter was exactly 10% of your ping.
Fixed your ping showing a green 0 before the first measurement, and keeping the previous server's ping after loading into a new mission.
